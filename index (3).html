CASE 01Digital Business School

From website to pipeline, for five brands.

Situation

Five school brands were generating leads through separate websites, forms and campaigns, but Sales didn't trust the handoff: MQLs weren't reliably ready to buy, and no one owned the full path from site to CRM.

Task

Own the full demand gen chain across all five brands, from the websites and forms to HubSpot and Odoo, so Sales could trust what landed in their queue.

Action

Owned the chain end to end: WordPress sites and OVH configuration, secured forms, a CRM architecture designed from the ground up, 45K+ contacts cleaned, a B2B/B2C scoring model, 8 nurture workflows, automated Sales alerts and Looker reporting on the whole pipeline.

Example: the "back to school" enrollment campaign

  • Built and maintained the landing pages and program sites for each brand in WordPress and Elementor, with hand-coded HTML/CSS where templates fell short.
  • Handled the OVH side directly: domains, DNS records, subdomains and 301 redirects, so every campaign URL resolved cleanly and old pages kept their traffic.
  • Set up lead forms with security in mind (SSL, anti-spam, GDPR consent fields), mapped every field into HubSpot, and briefed developers whenever a deeper integration or API sync was needed.
  • Contributed to the paid side of the enrollment push on Meta and TikTok, with Google Ads in support, and a UTM structure feeding source data straight into the CRM.
  • Applied the B2B/B2C scoring model in real time as forms came in, and routed every hand-raiser to Sales the moment they crossed the SQL threshold, cutting response lag from days to minutes.
  • Built an email nurture sequence for undecided prospects, running in parallel with an SMS reminder track for open house dates and application deadlines.
  • Managed a work-study marketer on communication, social content, Meta and TikTok Ads, and designed small logos and visual refreshes when a program needed its own identity.
  • Reported weekly on cost per lead and MQL to SQL conversion by channel and by brand, so spend shifted mid-campaign toward what was actually converting.
Result

Sales started acting on MQLs instead of second-guessing them.

CASE 02Fondation Horizon & Beyond

Launching a white-label offer to named accounts.

Situation

A white-label MOOC needed to reach specific institutions, banks and financial bodies, where generic outreach would have reached the wrong audience entirely.

Task

Set up the white-label offer and a focused B2B prospection strategy around it, built on account-level targeting instead of broad campaign reach.

Action

Built the CRM for the offer from scratch in HubSpot, configured the white-label setup, ran ABM targeting and B2B sequences for named institutional prospects, and kept account-level activity visible from first contact to conversion.

Example: from event floor to named-account sequence

  • Designed the logo and visual identity of the MOOC de la finance durable, then configured the white-label environment: partner-branded instance, custom subdomain and DNS on OVH.
  • Designed the HubSpot structure from scratch (properties, account tiers, deal pipeline), then built the named-account list of target institutions (banks and financial bodies) before any outreach started.
  • Collected contacts directly at finance and banking sector events, tagging each one at capture with their institution and role instead of dumping them into a generic list.
  • Enrolled each contact into an account-level sequence, not an individual one, so messaging stayed consistent across everyone from the same institution.
  • Triggered follow-up content based on engagement signals: a content download moved a contact forward, silence moved them into a lighter re-engagement track.
  • Rolled account-level engagement up into a single CRM view, so it was always clear which institutions were warming up and which had gone quiet.
Result

12 target accounts activated in six months, with engagement well above typical B2B benchmarks.

CASE 03Scorp INC

Getting three apps noticed.

Situation

Three B2B mobile apps were entering their market with little brand awareness and no structured way to turn interest into leads.

Task

Build awareness and a steady lead flow for the three apps, and put the first CRM basics in place to follow up on them.

Action

Ran brand awareness and demand gen for the three apps: positioning, content, social media and launch campaigns, paid social lead gen with dedicated landing pages, a visual rebranding, and my first CRM setup in Brevo and Salesforce.

Example: from launch post to qualified lead

  • Defined the positioning and a content calendar for each app: LinkedIn, Instagram, newsletters and product launch posts.
  • Ran lead gen campaigns on paid social and LinkedIn, each pointing to a dedicated landing page and lead form.
  • Supported the visual rebranding of the three apps (logo refinements, store visuals, email templates) so the brand looked the same everywhere.
  • Worked with the developers on app store pages and basic event tracking, to see which channels actually brought active users.
  • Set up my first CRM workflows: Brevo onboarding and reactivation emails, and a simple Salesforce lead pipeline so Sales could follow up on every inbound lead.
Result

The apps gained visibility in their market, and Sales received a regular flow of inbound leads instead of ad-hoc contacts.
